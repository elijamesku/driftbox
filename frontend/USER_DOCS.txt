--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The dashboard is a policy-driven infrastructure control plane. It gives you continuous governance across your cloud lifecycle: see drift, policy compliance, change risk, cost, and audit in one place.

Main areas:
- Overview (home): high-level health, control plane status, lifecycle governance, DigitalOcean (if connected), drift, policy, risk, cost, and recent activity.
- IDE: in-browser workspace to work with repos and AI assistance.
- Changes: track and manage infrastructure changes.
- Policies: view and manage policy rules and compliance.
- Governance: governance and compliance status.
- Sandbox: safe environment to test changes.
- Audit Logs: full history of actions and events.
- Cost: infrastructure cost tracking and trends.
- Teams: organize people and collaborate on projects.
- Repos: connected repositories.
- Settings: account, integrations (e.g. GitHub, DigitalOcean), and preferences.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GETTING STARTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Sign in with your account (GitHub or email, depending on your setup).
- In Settings, connect GitHub and optionally DigitalOcean to sync repos and infrastructure.
- The main dashboard (Overview) shows a summary once connected; use the sidebar to open specific sections.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW (HOME)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Overview page shows:

- Top stats: Managed Resources, Active Drift, Policy Violations, Est. Monthly Cost.
- Top Issues: cards for issue types (e.g. Security Misconfigurations, Infrastructure Drift, Cost Anomalies, Policy Violations) with open/total counts and trend.
- Control Plane Architecture: status of layers (Audit & Evidence, Runtime Drift Detection, Risk Assessment, State Reasoning, Policy Evaluation, Terraform Interaction).
- Lifecycle Governance: Pre-Deployment, Deployment, Post-Deployment pass rates and recent checks.
- DigitalOcean (if connected): resource inventory, cost breakdown, health, live resource status; Sync refreshes data.
- Infrastructure Drift Detection: chart of detected vs resolved drift; list of recent drift items (resource, field, expected vs actual, severity).
- Policy Compliance: compliance by category (e.g. Security, Cost, Network); recent violations with rule, resource, severity.
- Change Risk Assessment: risk distribution (Low/Medium/High/Critical); pending changes with blast radius and approval status.
- Infrastructure Cost: cost trend over time vs budget; current cost and change vs last period.
- Audit Trail: recent activity (who did what, when).
- Quick stats footer: Connected Repos, Active Teams, Sandbox Pass Rate, Avg. Resolution Time.

Clicking items (e.g. drift, policy violations, pending changes) opens a detail panel with actions (e.g. Reconcile, Approve, Reject). Use the Refresh button to reload data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The IDE is an in-browser workspace. Use it to:

- Browse and edit files from connected repositories.
- Use AI-assisted chat for code and infrastructure questions.
- Stage and commit changes (staging panel).
- Work with Terraform and other repo content.

Access it from the sidebar: IDE. The top bar may show chat/open controls depending on the view.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Changes section is for tracking infrastructure changes (e.g. pull requests, plan/apply). Use it to see what’s in flight, what’s approved, and what’s deployed. Exact workflows depend on your GitHub/integration setup.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POLICIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Policies define rules (security, cost, network, identity, data, etc.). The Policies page lets you:

- View active policies and their scope.
- See compliance status and violations.
- Remediate or suppress violations (from Overview detail panels or from the Policies page if available).

Violations show rule name, affected resource, severity, category, and suggested remediation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Governance & Compliance shows how well your infrastructure meets policy across the lifecycle (pre-deploy, deploy, post-deploy). You see pass rates, failed checks, and where to take action. Overview’s Lifecycle Governance panel is a short summary; the Governance page is the full view.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SANDBOX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Sandbox is a safe environment to run plans, tests, or experiments without affecting production. Use it to validate Terraform or policy changes before applying them. Overview may show a Sandbox Pass Rate; the Sandbox page is where you run and inspect those runs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AUDIT LOGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Audit Logs give a full history of actions: who did what, when, and on which resource or change. Use it for compliance, debugging, and review. The Overview’s Audit Trail shows recent activity; the Audit Logs page shows the full log (with filters if implemented).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Cost shows infrastructure cost over time and vs budget. You see estimated monthly cost, trend (e.g. last 6 months), and breakdown by resource type when DigitalOcean (or other providers) are connected. Use it to track spend and stay within budget.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEAMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Teams let you organize people and collaborate on infrastructure projects. You can create teams, invite members, and scope work (repos, policies, etc.) per team. Team pages may include a wiki/docs area for that team’s repos.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REPOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Repos lists your connected repositories (e.g. from GitHub). From here you manage which repos are linked, view status, and open them in the IDE or team context.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Settings covers:

- Account: profile, email, GitHub username.
- Integrations: connect or disconnect GitHub, DigitalOcean, etc. Connecting DigitalOcean enables the Overview’s DigitalOcean panel, cost data, and sync.
- Other preferences (theme, notifications, etc.) if available.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Refresh (Overview): reloads dashboard and integration data.
- Sync (DigitalOcean panel): refreshes DO resource and cost data.
- Detail panels: click drift items, policy violations, or pending changes on Overview to open actions (Reconcile, Approve, Reject, etc.).
- Sidebar: Dashboard (Overview), IDE, Changes, Policies, Governance, Sandbox, Audit Logs, Cost, Teams, Repos, Settings. Search and Documentation (if you add an in-app docs link) are also in the sidebar.
